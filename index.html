Welcome to Zuriel

Zuriel International Ministries is committed to serving God, building believers and uplifting the next generation through the knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.

Our desire is to raise Kingdom builders who will influence their generation through faith, worship, prayer and the Word of God.
