About Our Ministry

Discover our foundation, vision and commitment to the work of God's Kingdom.

Zuriel International Ministries is a Christian ministry established on the foundation of Jesus Christ. We are committed to building believers through the Word of God, prayer, worship and fellowship.

Our desire is to see lives transformed, families strengthened and believers equipped to serve God faithfully.

We believe that Jesus Christ is the foundation of our faith, our ministry and everything we do.
